Add Woodstock to your home screen

Woodstock is an early prototype. Every person, account and number in it is made up. Do not enter a real password or real banking details.

Open Woodstock

iPhone

  1. Open this link in Safari (not inside another app's browser).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Woodstock from its icon. It runs full screen, like an app.

Android

  1. Open this link in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app when it appears, or open the menu (three dots) and tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Woodstock from its icon.

Good to know

  1. Your demo account is saved on your phone only. Each new sign-up gets its own made-up numbers.
  2. To pick up a new version, close Woodstock fully and open it again.
  3. To start over, delete the home screen icon and clear the site data for this address in your browser settings.
